How to Play

  1. Tap Start Game to load a random Library of Babel page.
  2. Highlight any real English word (3–20 letters).
  3. Tap Check Word (or press C). A correct word stops the timer. A wrong word adds 60 s and loads a new page.
  4. Next Page (or N) skips the page with a 60 s penalty.
  5. Long words earn a bonus of −5 s for every letter after the 4th.
